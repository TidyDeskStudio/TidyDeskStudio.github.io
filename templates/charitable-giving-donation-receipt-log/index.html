Charitable Giving & Donation Receipt Log

Every December you scramble. Keep a running record of what you gave and which receipts are still missing.

Get it on Gumroad

Come the end of the year you want to know what you gave, to whom, and whether you have the paperwork. Most people end up searching old emails and the bottom of a bag.

This spreadsheet keeps a running log. Add each cash or goods donation as you make it, record the receipt status and the sheet flags every gift that still needs a receipt chased. A Year-End Summary tab totals the year, ready to print or hand to whoever prepares your records. It is a record-keeping tool, not tax or legal advice.

What it does

  • Dashboard with a receipts-to-chase list, receipt ring, biggest gifts and a giving-goal bar
  • Gifts log for cash and goods with description, value and receipt status
  • Set your own receipt amount; gifts at or above it show Chase receipt until the receipt arrives
  • Charities tab with cause, registration number, contact and automatic totals
  • Year-End Summary tab: cash, goods, total, receipts received and value without paperwork
  • Giving by month, by cause and by charity, plus a cause-by-month heatmap
  • Fill-in sample data so you can see every feature before you add your own
  • Plain-English quick start guide included

What you get

  • Charitable Giving & Donation Receipt Log (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
