Mortgage & Loan Extra Payment Payoff Calculator

See how much sooner and cheaper your loan ends when you add a little extra each month.

Get it on Gumroad

A small extra payment can feel pointless until you see what it does over years. Most lender statements will not show you, and doing the sums by hand is slow and easy to get wrong.

Enter your loan amount, rate, term and the extra you could add each month, plus an optional one-off lump sum. This spreadsheet builds a full month-by-month schedule for the normal plan and the plan with extras side by side, then shows the payoff date, months saved and interest saved. A scenarios table compares different extra amounts, and the dashboard adds charts, milestones and a monthly review checklist. It is a calculator and record-keeping tool using the figures you type in. It is not financial advice.

What it does

  • Full payment schedule for up to 40 years, built with standard payment functions
  • Normal plan and plan with extras side by side, month by month
  • Optional one-off lump sum in any payment number
  • Scenarios table comparing extra amounts: time to payoff, interest and months saved
  • Loan Summary tab with regular payment, payoff dates and totals
  • Dashboard with charts, alerts, goals, milestones and a monthly review checklist
  • As-of date shows balance and share of the loan repaid to date
  • Sample data, clickable navigation and a Quick Start Guide included
