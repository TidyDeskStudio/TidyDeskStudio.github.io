Landlord Rental Property Tracker

Know which rentals actually make money - rent, late payments, expenses and yield per property.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

If you manage your own rentals, the numbers usually live in bank statements, receipts and memory. This workbook pulls them into one place: log the rent each month, log each expense against a property, and the dashboard does the rest.

You'll see rent collected, expenses and net income for the year, who is late and by how many days, leases that need renewing, and a side-by-side of income vs costs for every property - plus yield on purchase price.

What it does

  • Rent log with automatic Paid / Part paid / Late / Due status and days late
  • Expense log by property and category - ready for tax time
  • Per-property rent collected, expenses, net income and yield
  • Lease end alerts: Active, Renew soon (60 days), Expired
  • Dashboard: 8 key numbers, monthly rent vs expenses chart, income vs costs per property, expenses by category
  • Handles 1 to 50+ properties; edit categories and payment methods on the Lists tab
  • Any currency

What you get

  • Landlord Rental Property Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
