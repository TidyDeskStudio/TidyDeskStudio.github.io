Bridal & Event Makeup Booking, Trial & Deposit Tracker

Price every booking per face, track trials, deposits and balances, and see what each client still owes you.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Wedding and event bookings come with moving parts: a bride and her party at different prices, an early-start fee, travel, a trial, a deposit and a balance due before the day. Keeping that in messages and notes makes it easy to lose track of who has paid what.

This spreadsheet gives you a booking log that prices each package per face, adds early-start and travel fees, splits the total into deposit and balance, and shows the balance due date and what is still owed. A trials tab shows which trial clients have not booked yet, and the dashboard shows income, money to chase and your goals. It is a record-keeping tool, not tax or legal advice.

What it does

  • Dashboard with biggest bookings, a paid-in-full ring, goal progress bars and a money-to-chase list
  • Booking log with price per face, early-start fee, travel fee and extras added up automatically
  • Deposit, balance, balance due date and amount still owed worked out for every booking
  • Payment check: Paid in full, Balance due, Deposit needed or Balance overdue
  • Packages tab with price and minutes per face, plus income per chair hour
  • Trials tab that flags trial clients who have not booked yet
  • Income by event type, lead source and month, with a bookings heatmap
  • Dropdowns, sample data and a Quick Start Guide included

What you get

  • Bridal & Event Makeup Booking, Trial & Deposit Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
