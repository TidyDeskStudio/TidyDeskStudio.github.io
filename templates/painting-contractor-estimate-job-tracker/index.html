Painting Contractor Estimate Calculator & Job Tracker

Turn room measurements into gallons, labor hours and a consistent quote - then track each job from quote to paid.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Most painting quotes start with a tape measure and end with a guess. Two similar rooms get two different prices, the paint runs short, and you only find out the job lost money after it's done.

This workbook prices every surface the same way. Enter length, height, openings, coats and the condition of the surface, and it works out paintable area, gallons, painting and prep hours from your own production rates, materials, cost and a price that includes your overhead, contingency and profit. Lines add up into a job quote. Then track each job through Quoted, Won, In progress, Complete or Lost, enter actual hours and materials, and see gross profit after labor and materials and whether the crew came in on target. Your rates are editable, so the numbers match how you work.

What it does

  • Room-by-room estimate lines: area minus doors and windows, coats, surface condition
  • Gallons from coverage, labor hours from your own sq ft per hour plus prep time by condition
  • Price built from crew cost, materials, sundries, overhead %, contingency % and profit %
  • Editable production rates and paint prices for walls, ceilings, trim, doors, cabinets and siding
  • Job tracker with stages, follow-up alerts on old quotes, Paid / Unpaid status
  • Quote vs actual: gross profit after labor and materials, plus an Over / On target / Under hours check
  • Quick Room Estimate calculator for a fast price on the phone
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, quoted vs won by month, work by job type, paint and hours by surface

What you get

  • Painting Contractor Estimate Calculator & Job Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
