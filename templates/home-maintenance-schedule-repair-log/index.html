Home Maintenance Schedule & Repair Log

A simple home maintenance schedule with repair records, contractors and warranty alerts.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Most home repairs get expensive because a small job was missed: gutters, filters, a boiler service, a leak that wasn't fixed early. This workbook tells you what's due and keeps a record of everything you've had done.

List your regular jobs with how often they need doing and when you last did them - the next due date and an Overdue / Due soon / OK status are worked out for you. Log repairs with the contractor and cost, keep your trusted tradespeople in one list, and get a heads-up before appliance warranties end. The dashboard shows what needs attention and where your money goes.

What it does

  • Maintenance schedule with automatic next due date and Overdue / Due soon / OK status
  • Choose how many days' warning you want
  • Repair log with area, contractor, cost and guarantee date
  • Contractor list with rating, jobs done and total spent
  • Appliance warranties with Active / Ends soon / Expired alerts
  • Dashboard: overdue and upcoming tasks, repair spend, upkeep due, warranties ending
  • Charts: repair spending by month and area, tasks by area

What you get

  • Home Maintenance Schedule & Repair Log (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
