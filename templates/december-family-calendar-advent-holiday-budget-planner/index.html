December Family Calendar & Holiday Budget Planner

School concerts, parties, baking days, gifts and travel all land in the same four weeks, and by the 20th two events clash and the budget is gone.

This spreadsheet gives you an events list with date, time, who it is for, budget line and cost, a Clash flag when the same person has two things on one date, and a Soon or Missed flag as dates approach. An advent tab holds a small activity for each day, a spending log records purchases, and a budget tab adds what you have spent to what is still planned and shows On budget, Near limit or Over for each line. The dashboard adds a December calendar, family spotlight, prep checklist, alerts and charts. It is a family planning and record-keeping tool and not financial advice.

What it does

  • Events list with date, time, type, who, budget line, planned cost and status
  • Clash flag when the same person has two events on one date, plus Soon and Missed flags
  • Advent tab with an activity for each day from 1 to 24 and a Done, To do or Missed status
  • Spending log by budget line and person
  • Budget tab that adds spent and still-planned costs and shows On budget, Near limit or Over
  • Dashboard with month and person dropdowns, a December calendar and a tick-box prep checklist
  • Alerts plus charts for budget vs committed, events by type and spending by month
  • Sample data, clickable navigation and a Quick Start Guide included
