Monthly Bill Due-Date Calendar & Autopay Checker

Every bill by day of the month, an autopay check and a clear list of what needs action this week.

Get it on Gumroad

Bills arrive on different days, some are on autopay and some are not, and the one you forgot is always the one with a fee. A checklist on paper does not tell you which payments are about to land or whether the autopay actually went through.

This spreadsheet keeps one list of your bills with the due day, amount, account and autopay setting. It shows each bill's status from today's date, a day-by-day calendar of what is due and what you pay by hand, and a payment log that flags late fees and amounts above the usual. It is a record-keeping tool for your own bills, not financial advice.

What it does

  • Bill list with due day, amount, account and autopay setting
  • Automatic status: Overdue, Due soon, Check autopay, Autopay soon, Upcoming, Autopay or Paid
  • Calendar tab for all 31 days with total due, autopay amount, pay-by-hand amount and running total
  • Heavy day flag using a limit you set
  • Payment log comparing each payment with the usual amount, with late-fee and higher-than-usual flags
  • Dashboard with heaviest days, paid-so-far gauge, category chart and needs-action list
  • Bills as a share of income and share on autopay
  • Editable categories and accounts lists

What you get

  • Monthly Bill Due-Date Calendar & Autopay Checker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
