Bookkeeper Multi-Client Month-End Close Status Board

See every client's month-end close on one board - steps done, documents missing and closes running late.

Get it on Payhip

With a dozen clients, month-end turns into a juggling act: whose bank feeds are done, who still owes you a statement, and which close is already past the date you promised.

This board gives each client-month one row with six standard close steps. Set each step as you work and the sheet works out how complete the close is and whether it's In progress, Waiting on client, Due soon, Late or Closed. A Documents tab tracks every statement and receipt you've asked for and flags the ones to chase. The dashboard shows late closes, outstanding documents and how many days after month end you usually finish. It is a workflow tracker only - no accounting or tax advice.

What it does

  • Close Board: one row per client per month with six step columns and dropdown statuses
  • Automatic Complete %, steps done and N/A handling
  • Status that works itself out: Not started, In progress, Waiting on client, Due soon, Late, Closed
  • Per-client close-by day, or your own default
  • Documents tab with chase dates and a Chase now flag
  • Clients tab with open closes, late closes and documents outstanding
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, closes by month, open closes and missing documents by client

What you get

  • Bookkeeper Multi-Client Month-End Close Status Board (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
