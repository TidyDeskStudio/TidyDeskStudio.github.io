Dog Grooming Appointment & Client Tracker

Every dog, owner, coat note and appointment in one place - with rebook dates and no-show tracking.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Paper cards and phone notes work until you can't remember which blade you used on Biscuit or whether Max's owner paid last time. This tracker keeps every dog and every appointment in one place.

Add each dog with owner details, size, coat and temperament notes and how often they should come back. Log appointments with price, add-ons and tips. Finished grooms get a rebook-by date, unpaid grooms and upcoming bookings are flagged, and no-shows are counted. The dashboard shows revenue, tips, grooms done, no-show rate and which services and dog sizes bring in the most.

What it does

  • Pet records: owner, phone, breed, size, coat notes, temperament and health notes
  • Appointment log with price, add-ons, tips, status and payment
  • Rebook-by date for every finished groom based on each dog's interval
  • Alerts: Unpaid, No-show, Upcoming
  • Per-dog grooms done, total spent and no-shows
  • Dashboard: revenue, tips, grooms, average per groom, no-show rate, unpaid, upcoming
  • Charts: revenue and grooms by month, revenue by service, grooms by dog size

What you get

  • Dog Grooming Appointment & Client Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
