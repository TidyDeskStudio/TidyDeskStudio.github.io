Rental Turnover Cleaning Schedule & Pay Tracker

Track every checkout clean, what each host owes you and what to restock - built for turnover cleaners.

Get it on Payhip

You clean several rentals for several hosts. Checkouts come in at short notice, some are same-day, you charge for restock items and laundry, and each host pays on different terms. Keeping it all in your head, or in a notes app, means missed charges and late payments.

This tracker gives every turnover one row. Pick the property and the host, rate, laundry fee and same-day surcharge are applied for you. It shows your real hourly rate on each clean, warns when a host is late paying, totals a host's month on a statement tab and tells you which supplies to reorder.

What it does

  • Turnover log with automatic host lookup, fee and per-hour rate
  • Same-day flag and surcharge when the next guest checks in the same day
  • Laundry fee, restock charges and agreed extras added to the fee
  • Status per clean: Scheduled, To be paid, Overdue, Paid - using each host's own payment terms
  • Host Statement tab: one host's month in a few numbers
  • Supplies tab with reorder alerts and stock value
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly billed vs still owed, billed and owed by host, cleans by property

What you get

  • Rental Turnover Cleaning Schedule & Pay Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
