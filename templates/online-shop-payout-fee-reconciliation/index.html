Online Shop Payout & Fee Reconciliation Sheet

Your payouts never match your sales. See every fee, refund and shortfall and find out where the money went.

Get it on Gumroad

You sell in more than one place, each one pays out on its own schedule, and the amount in the bank never quite matches the sales report. Working out why means opening statements, a calculator and a lot of patience.

This spreadsheet does the arithmetic. Add each payout statement with its gross sales, refunds, fees and deductions, then the amount that reached your bank. The sheet works out what you should have received, flags short and over payments, compares the fees charged with the rate you expected for each channel and totals everything by month. It is a record-keeping tool, not accounting or tax advice.

What it does

  • Dashboard with a payouts-to-look-at list, a match ring, biggest differences and a sales-goal bar
  • One row per payout with gross sales, refunds, fees, other deductions and the amount received
  • Expected net payout and variance worked out for every row
  • Check column: Matched, Short, Over or Awaiting payout, with your own rounding tolerance
  • Fee check against the fee rate you expect from each channel
  • Channels tab with effective fee rate, refunds and net variance per channel
  • Monthly summary tab for gross sales, refunds, fees, money received and variance
  • Generic Channel A, B and C names you can rename to your own sales channels

What you get

  • Online Shop Payout & Fee Reconciliation Sheet (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
