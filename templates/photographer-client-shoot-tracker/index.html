Photographer Client Booking & Shoot Tracker

Every inquiry, shoot, deposit and gallery deadline in one place - so nothing slips.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Photography admin is easy to lose track of: inquiries waiting for a reply, deposits, balances due after the shoot, and galleries you promised by a certain date. This tracker keeps every client in one row and tells you what needs attention.

Move each client from Inquiry to Booked, Shot, Editing and Delivered. The delivery due date is set from your usual turnaround, and alerts flag late galleries, galleries due this week and balances still owed. The dashboard shows booked work, money received and which shoot types and sources bring in the most.

What it does

  • Client pipeline: Inquiry, Booked, Shot, Editing, Delivered, Lost
  • Package price, deposit and balance with an automatic amount owed
  • Gallery delivery due date from your turnaround time
  • Alerts: Gallery late, Due this week, Balance owed, Reply / quote
  • Expense log for gear, software and travel
  • Dashboard: booked work, received, owed, profit, upcoming shoots, open inquiries, average package
  • Charts: booked work by month, income by shoot type, where clients come from

What you get

  • Photographer Client Booking & Shoot Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
