Content Creator Brand Deal Tracker

Keep every sponsorship organised - from first pitch to paid invoice.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Brand deals come in through email, DMs and agencies, and the admin piles up fast: what you promised, when it's due, whether you invoiced, and who still hasn't paid. This tracker keeps it all in one row per deal.

Move each deal through the stages from Pitched to Paid. The sheet works out payment due dates from your terms, flags overdue content and late payments, and calculates your take-home after any agent fee. The dashboard shows income received, money waiting, confirmed work and your pipeline.

What it does

  • Deal pipeline: Pitched, Negotiating, Confirmed, Content delivered, Invoiced, Paid, Lost
  • Deliverables, content due date and posting date for every deal
  • Automatic payment due date from your payment terms
  • Alerts for overdue content and late payments
  • Take-home after agent / manager %
  • Dashboard: income received, waiting to be paid, confirmed work, pipeline value, average deal, alerts
  • Charts: income by month, deals by stage, earnings by platform

What you get

  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
