Twelve slots in a day, a handful of unpaid deposits and a stack of galleries you promised in a week: mini-session season is when the admin quietly piles up.

This spreadsheet gives you a session log with time, family, location, package, fee, add-on sales, deposit and balance, a gallery due date worked out from your turnaround, and a Flag that shows Deposit due, Balance due, Gallery due soon, Gallery overdue, In editing or Done. A costs tab and a locations tab show revenue, costs and profit per place. The dashboard adds a session calendar, location spotlight, session-day checklist, alerts and charts. It is a record-keeping tool and not legal, contract or tax advice.
