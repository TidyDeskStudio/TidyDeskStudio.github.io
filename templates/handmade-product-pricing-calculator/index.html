Handmade Product Pricing Calculator

Stop guessing your prices. Turn materials, time and fees into wholesale and retail prices that actually pay you.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Most makers underprice because they forget their time, their overheads or the fees taken off every sale. This calculator builds your price from the ground up.

Enter what your supplies cost once, list what goes into each product, and add how long it takes to make. The workbook works out materials, labour, overhead and total cost, suggests a wholesale and a retail price, and shows your real profit and margin after selling fees - with a warning when a product is losing money or the margin is thin.

What it does

  • Materials list that works out cost per gram, metre or piece from pack prices
  • Recipes tab: list what goes into each product - material costs add up automatically
  • Labour at your own hourly rate, plus overhead %
  • Suggested wholesale and retail prices from your markups
  • Profit and margin after marketplace and payment fees, with Healthy / Thin / Losing money checks
  • Quick Price Check calculator for one-off ideas, including your break-even price
  • Dashboard with average cost, price, profit and margin by category

What you get

  • Handmade Product Pricing Calculator (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)
