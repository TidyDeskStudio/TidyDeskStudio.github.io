Side Hustle Income & Expense Tracker

A free spreadsheet to log side income and costs, see profit by month and quarter, and keep a set-aside amount in view.

Get it on Gumroad

Side income is easy to earn and easy to lose track of. Payments arrive in different places, receipts end up in a drawer, and at the end of the year nobody can say what came in, what went out or what is left.

This is a free download. Log each payment and each cost on its own row, pick a source or category from a dropdown, and the sheet adds up income, expenses and profit by month and by quarter. A set-aside percentage that you choose shows how much of your profit to keep to one side. The dashboard also lists unpaid invoices, watches your budgets and shows a bookkeeping routine you can tick off. It is a record-keeping tool and not tax, legal or financial advice.

What it does

  • Income log with source and Received, Pending or Overdue status
  • Expense log with category and a saved-receipt flag
  • Month by month table of money in, money out, profit and set-aside amount
  • Set-aside percentage you choose, used as simple arithmetic only
  • Quarterly summary cards for Q1 to Q4 plus income by source and quarter
  • Command-wall dashboard with calendar, charts, alerts and goal tracking
  • Month, source and quarter dropdowns that change the dashboard
  • Sample data, instructions and a Quick Start Guide included

What you get

  • Side Hustle Income & Expense Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
