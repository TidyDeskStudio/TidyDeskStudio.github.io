Volunteer Hours & Shift Tracker

Track volunteer shifts and hours by person, program and month - ready for grant reports.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Funders and boards want to know how many volunteer hours went into your work, and volunteers deserve to be thanked for theirs. Pulling those numbers from sign-in sheets and emails at year end is painful. This tracker keeps them up to date as you go.

Log each shift in one row. Every volunteer gets hours this year, total hours, shift count and a milestone badge at 20, 50 and 100+ hours. The dashboard shows hours by month and program, top volunteers, new volunteers this year and the value of volunteer time at the hourly rate you choose.

What it does

  • Shift log with date, volunteer, program, hours and notes
  • Volunteer roster with hours this year, total hours, shifts and milestones
  • Value of volunteer time at your chosen hourly rate
  • Dashboard: hours, value, shifts, active volunteers, hours this month, new volunteers, milestones
  • Charts: hours by month, by program, by volunteer
  • Rename programs to match your organisation
  • Built for adult volunteers - keep personal details minimal

What you get

  • Volunteer Hours & Shift Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
