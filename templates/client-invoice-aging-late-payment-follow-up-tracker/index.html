Client Invoice Aging & Late Payment Follow-Up Tracker

Know who owes you, how late it is and when to chase, with aging buckets, partial payments and a chase list that builds itself.

Get it on Gumroad

Chasing late invoices is hard when the details live in your head, your inbox and three different notes. You lose track of who was reminded, who paid half and which invoice has quietly slipped past 60 days.

This spreadsheet gives every invoice one row. Enter the issue date, terms, amount and what has been paid so far, and it works out the due date, balance, days late, aging bucket and a status from Not due to Chase now. A Next chase date is calculated from the gap you choose. The Clients tab totals what each client owes and marks them Good, Watch or High risk. The dashboard shows aging buckets, the chase-now list, biggest balances and a client spotlight. It is a record-keeping tool and not legal or financial advice.

What it does

  • Aging buckets: current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 and over 90 days late
  • Partial payments: type what has been paid and the balance updates
  • Status per invoice: Not due, Due soon, Late, Chase now or Paid
  • Next chase date worked out from the gap you set, plus a chase step dropdown
  • Client tab with totals, average days late and a Good / Watch / High risk label
  • Dashboard with month and client dropdowns, a due-date calendar and weekly follow-up checklist
  • Late fee estimate per invoice using a rate you control
  • Alerts, collection goals, awards, charts and a client by bucket matrix
  • Sample data, clickable navigation and a Quick Start Guide included

What you get

  • Client Invoice Aging & Late Payment Follow-Up Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
