Every January the good intentions arrive: clear the cupboards, set some goals, finally keep a routine. By February the lists are scattered and nobody knows what is done.

This free spreadsheet gives you a Zones tab for declutter jobs with room, size, owner, planned date, items out and hours, a Goals tab with targets, progress bars and an On track, Due soon, Behind or Done status, and a Routines tab that shows whether each daily, weekly or monthly routine is Keeping up or Slipping. The dashboard adds a calendar, family spotlight, reset checklist, alerts and charts. It is a household planning tool and not financial or professional advice.
