Household Important Documents & Accounts Locator

A locator for your family's important papers, accounts and contacts - with renewal and backup flags, and no passwords stored.

Get it on Gumroad

If something happened tomorrow, would your family know where the insurance policy, the deeds or the passports are, who to call, or which accounts even exist? For most households the honest answer is that it all lives in one person's head.

This spreadsheet is a locator, not a vault. For every paper and account you record where it is, whose it is, who to call and when it renews, and the file flags what has expired, what is due soon, what has no digital copy and which accounts have no backup person who knows about them. It deliberately has no columns for passwords, PINs or account numbers. It is a record-keeping tool, not legal, financial or estate advice.

What it does

  • Document register with category, owner, where it is kept, provider and contact
  • Renewal flags: Expired, Due soon, Upcoming, using a window you set
  • Review flags so papers are re-checked on the schedule you choose
  • Accounts list with provider, holder, how to get access, monthly and yearly cost
  • Backup-person check that shows accounts nobody else knows about
  • Contacts tab for the agent, solicitor, accountant and a trusted person, with check-in reminders
  • Renewal Check and Family Overview tabs for quick answers
  • Dashboard with costliest accounts, renewal chart, progress bars and action lists
  • No password, PIN or account-number columns by design
