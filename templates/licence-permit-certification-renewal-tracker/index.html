Licence, Permit & Insurance Renewal Tracker

Track every licence, permit, certificate and insurance policy, see days left to expiry, and plan renewal fees before anything lapses.

Get it on Gumroad

Licences, permits, insurance policies and certificates all expire on different dates, with different offices and different fees. Miss one and work can stop, so most owners end up with reminders scattered across emails, diaries and a drawer of letters.

This spreadsheet gives every item one row with its issuer, owner, reference number, expiry date, renewal fee and renewal step. Days left, a Current, Due soon, Urgent or Expired status and the yearly cost work themselves out. A renewals log tracks fees paid and missing receipts, and the dashboard shows expiries on a calendar, a 90-day fee forecast, alerts and charts. It is a record-keeping tool and not legal advice: always confirm dates and rules with the issuing body.

What it does

  • Register tab with type, issuer, owner, reference number, issue date, expiry date and renewal fee
  • Days left and a colour-coded Current, Due soon, Urgent or Expired status that update every day
  • Reminder lead time and urgent window you can change on the Start Here tab
  • Yearly cost worked out from each fee and renewal cycle, checked against a yearly budget
  • Renewals log that tracks fees paid and flags renewals with no receipt
  • Dashboard with a month and person dropdown, expiry calendar, 90-day fee forecast and tick-box weekly routine
  • Charts for fees by month, yearly cost by type and issuer, and a heatmap of fees by expiry month
  • Sample data, clickable navigation and a Quick Start Guide included

What you get

  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)
