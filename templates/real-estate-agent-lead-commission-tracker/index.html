Real Estate Agent Lead & Commission Tracker

Every lead, follow-up and commission in one sheet - see what's coming and what you're owed.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Most agents don't lose deals because of the market - they lose them when a follow-up slips. This tracker keeps every lead and deal in one row, flags who to call, and shows what your pipeline is worth to you after the brokerage split.

Add leads with their source, side, area and price, move them through the stages and set a next follow-up date. Commission uses your default rate unless a deal has its own, and your share after the split is worked out. The dashboard shows pipeline, expected and earned commission, money still to receive and which lead sources actually close.

What it does

  • Lead and deal list with source, buyer/seller side, area and price
  • Stages from New lead to Under contract, Closed or Lost
  • Follow-up flags: Overdue, Today, Scheduled, Set a date
  • Gross commission and your share after the brokerage split (per-deal rate override)
  • Commission received vs still to receive
  • Dashboard: pipeline, expected, earned and received commission, deals closed, close rate, follow-ups due
  • Charts: commission by month, pipeline by stage, lead sources that close

What you get

  • Real Estate Agent Lead & Commission Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
