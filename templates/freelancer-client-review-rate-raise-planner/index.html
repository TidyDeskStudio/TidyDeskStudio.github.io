Freelancer Year-End Client Review & Rate Raise Planner

Review every client at year-end: effective hourly rate, payment speed and the rate raise to plan for next year.

Get it on Gumroad

At the end of the year most freelancers know what they earned but not which clients were actually worth it. A fixed-fee project that ran long, a client who always pays six weeks late and a retainer you never raised all hide inside the total.

This spreadsheet turns your invoices into a client-by-client review. Log each invoice with hours and amount billed and it shows your effective hourly rate per client, how many days each one takes to pay, who is below your target rate and who pays late. Then enter a proposed raise and your planned hours and it calculates the new rate and the extra income as a simple sum. It is a planning calculation, not a forecast, and it does not give financial advice.

What it does

  • Client list with current rate, payment terms, proposed raise % and raise status
  • Work Log with hours, amount billed, paid date and hourly yield per invoice
  • Effective hourly rate per client and a rank from best to worst paying
  • Days-to-pay and a payment check: On time, Paid late, Overdue or Awaiting
  • Review flag: Below target, Slow payer or On target
  • New rate and extra income from each planned raise, as a plain calculation
  • Raise Check tab to test one raise and see the hours you could lose and earn the same
  • Dashboard with best-paying clients, raises still to send, late invoices and billed by month
