HVAC Service Call & Maintenance Plan Tracker

Log service calls, track every unit's history and never miss a maintenance plan tune-up or renewal.

You sell yearly maintenance plans, but the tune-ups that are due live in your head, the serial numbers are on scraps of paper and nobody remembers which furnace has been repaired three times. Then a plan lapses without a renewal call and a repeat fault eats your margin.

This spreadsheet keeps customers, equipment, maintenance plans and service calls linked together. Each plan shows its end date, tune-ups done and left, and whether the next visit is Overdue or Due soon. Each call is priced from your labour rate with a plan discount, then shows profit, margin and a Low margin, OK or Strong flag, plus Callback when the same unit needs a repeat repair. It is a record-keeping and pricing tool; it gives no advice on refrigerant rules, safety or regulations.

What it does

  • Service call log with labour priced from your rate, emergency multiplier and plan discount
  • Billed amount, costs, profit and margin on every call, flagged Low margin, OK or Strong
  • Maintenance plan tracker with end date, tune-ups done and left, next visit due and Overdue or Due soon flags
  • Renew soon and Expired flags so no plan lapses unnoticed
  • Equipment register with serial, install date, age, warranty status and service history
  • Callback flag for repeat repairs on the same unit within 30 days
  • Payment status per call: Scheduled, To be paid, Overdue, Paid
  • Quote Check tab to test the margin before you commit to a price
  • Dashboard with biggest customers, goal bars, call-type heatmap and action lists
