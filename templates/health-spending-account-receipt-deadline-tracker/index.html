Your account balance resets at year end, and somewhere there is a pile of receipts you meant to claim. Without one place to look, money gets left behind and nobody remembers which claims went in.

This free spreadsheet keeps one row per receipt and works out the rest. Each receipt shows To claim, Claim soon, Overdue, Waiting, Paid back, Rejected or Not eligible, the Accounts tab shows the balance left for each person with a Use soon or Expired status, and a year-end steps list keeps the paperwork moving. The dashboard adds a claim-by calendar, a person spotlight, alerts and charts. It is a record-keeping tool only and not medical, tax or financial advice. Plan rules vary, so check with your plan provider.

What it does

  • Receipts tab with person, category, amount, eligibility and claim status dropdowns

  • Accounts tab with amount set aside, paid back, waiting and balance left per person
  • Use-it-by status: Plenty of time, Use soon, Expired or Fully used
