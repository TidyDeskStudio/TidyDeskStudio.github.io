Family Chore Rota, Points & Pocket Money Planner

Run the household chores with a colour-coded daily board, streaks, points, a family leaderboard and pocket money that works itself out.

Chores turn into arguments when nobody can see who did what. Sticker charts fall apart after a week, and working out pocket money by hand on Sunday night is a chore of its own.

This spreadsheet gives every day one row and every chore one dropdown. Pick who did the job, or mark it Skipped, and the board colours each person differently, counts the jobs, adds up the points you set for each chore, keeps a streak going and labels the day Full house, Steady, Slow or Rest day. The Family tab turns the points into pocket money for the children, a leaderboard and a goal bar for everyone. The dashboard shows best days, slow days, skipped chores and charts by month and weekday. It is a household organiser and not financial advice.

What it does

  • Checklist on the dashboard itself: tick boxes turn green, bars fill and a week ring updates live
  • Month and family-member dropdowns on the dashboard that redraw the calendar and spotlight
  • Calendar heat map showing each person's points day by day
  • Household health score, alerts, level and title for every person, and week-on-week trend
  • Family awards panel with progress bars that unlock as you hit streaks and point goals
  • Colour-coded daily board: pick who did each of ten chores or mark it Skipped
  • Reward shop: set prizes and point costs, log claims, and see who can afford what
  • Family tab with pocket money for the children, goal bars and a leaderboard
  • Clickable navigation, dropdowns, sample data and a Quick Start Guide included
