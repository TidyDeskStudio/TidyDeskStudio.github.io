Black Friday & Cyber Week Deal Planner and Price Tracker

Plan your sale-season shopping: target prices, deal deadlines, price history and a budget that warns you before you overspend.

Get it on Gumroad

Sale season is exciting and expensive. You have a list in your head, a dozen tabs open and no idea whether that deal is actually cheaper than last month. It is easy to buy things you did not need and miss the ones you did.

This spreadsheet keeps your whole wishlist in one place. For each item you enter the usual price and the price you are willing to pay, then add the deal price and end date when you spot one. The sheet works out the discount, tells you whether it hit your target, warns when a deal is about to end, tracks category budgets and shows how much you are saving. It is a personal planning tool and does not give financial advice.

What it does

  • Wishlist with category, priority, who it is for, store, usual price, target price and deal price
  • Automatic discount, saving and a deal check: Hit target, Good deal, Wait, Missed or No deal yet
  • Deal deadline flag that shows Ends soon and Ended
  • Price Log tab that marks the lowest price you have seen for each item
  • Category budgets with Nearly full and Over budget alerts
  • Stores tab with return days, price-match notes, planned spend and saving per store
  • Deal Check tab to compare one offer including delivery in seconds
  • Dashboard with ready-to-buy list, savings leaderboard, budget bars and status funnel
