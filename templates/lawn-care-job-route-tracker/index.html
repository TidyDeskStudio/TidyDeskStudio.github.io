Lawn Care Job, Route & Client Tracker

Clients, routes, quotes and unpaid jobs for your lawn care business - in one simple sheet.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Running a lawn round means juggling regulars, one-off jobs, quotes you're waiting on and people who still owe you. This tracker keeps it all straight without paying for software.

List your clients with their route day, how often you visit and their price. Log each visit and move it from Quoted to Booked, Done and Paid. The sheet flags jobs to invoice and quotes to chase, works out each client's weekly value, and the dashboard shows revenue, profit and how much each route day is worth.

What it does

  • Client list with address, service, frequency, route day, price and gate notes
  • Visit log with Quoted, Booked, Done, Paid and Cancelled status
  • Follow-up flags: Invoice (done but unpaid) and Chase quote (older than 7 days)
  • Weekly value per client and regular weekly income
  • Route-day view: how many clients and how much income each day brings
  • Expense log so you see profit, not just takings
  • Dashboard and charts: revenue vs expenses by month, revenue by service

What you get

  • Lawn Care Job, Route & Client Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
