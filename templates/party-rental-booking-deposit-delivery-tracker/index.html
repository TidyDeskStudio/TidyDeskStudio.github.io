Party Rental Booking, Deposit & Delivery Tracker

Track every party rental booking, catch double bookings, chase deposits and balances, and log damage in one tidy workbook.

A Saturday with two bookings on the same bounce house, a deposit nobody collected and a torn seam you forgot to log is an expensive day. Most rental businesses run on a calendar, a notebook and memory.

This spreadsheet gives you a booking log with delivery zone and slot, automatic totals, deposit and balance due, a Clash flag when an item is booked twice on one date, and a Deposit due or Balance due flag when payments are late. An items tab shows rentals, revenue, booked share and Ready or In repair status, and a damage log works out net repair cost after what you charge the customer. The dashboard adds a booking calendar, item spotlight, delivery-day checklist, alerts and charts. It is a record-keeping tool and not legal, insurance or tax advice.

What it does

  • Booking log with event date, customer, item, delivery zone, slot, fees, deposit and balance paid
  • Clash flag when the same item is booked twice on one date
  • Deposit due and Balance due flags that follow your own deposit percentage and lead time
  • Items tab with rentals, revenue, booked share and a Ready or In repair status
  • Damage log with severity, repair cost, amount charged to the customer and net cost
  • Dashboard with month and item dropdowns, an events calendar and a tick-box delivery-day checklist
  • Attention list, alerts and charts for revenue by month, item and delivery zone
  • Sample data, clickable navigation and a Quick Start Guide included
