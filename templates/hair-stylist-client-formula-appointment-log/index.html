Hair Stylist Client Formula & Appointment Log

Keep every client's colour formula, visit history and rebook date in one tidy log.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Scribbled formula cards go missing, and it's hard to tell which regulars haven't been back in a while. When a client sits in your chair asking for "the same as last time", you need the answer in seconds.

This log keeps a card for every client with hair notes, natural level, current formula, developer, processing time and patch test date. Each visit records the exact formula used, service price, retail and tips, and the client card shows last visit, days since and a Rebook status. An expenses tab covers booth rent and colour stock, so the dashboard can show what you actually take home each month.

What it does

  • Client cards: hair notes, natural level, current formula, developer, processing time and patch test date
  • Visit log with the exact formula used, service, price, retail sold and tip
  • Last visit, days since and Rebook status (Overdue, Due soon, OK) from each client's usual interval
  • Total spent and number of visits per client
  • Expenses tab for booth or suite rent, colour stock, tools and education
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly income vs expenses, income by service, expenses by category
  • Editable dropdowns for services, payment methods and expense categories

What you get

  • Hair Stylist Client Formula & Appointment Log (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
