Holiday Pre-Order, Pickup Slot & Bake-Day Planner

Take holiday pre-orders, cap your pickup slots and know exactly what to bake for each pickup day.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Holiday pre-orders pile up fast: messages, paper lists, half-paid deposits and no clear idea how many pies you need for Wednesday. Then the night before pickup you are counting by hand.

This planner keeps every order line in one tab and does the counting for you. Each pickup slot has its own cutoff date and capacity, so you can see when it is nearly full. The Menu tab totals how many of each item are needed for up to four pickup days and how many batches to bake, and the Batch Scaler multiplies any recipe. Payments, balances and pickups are tracked line by line, with clear statuses on the day.

What it does

  • Orders tab with automatic price, line total, balance and status (Ready, Unpaid, After cutoff, Collected, Not collected, No-show)
  • Pickup slots with order cutoff dates and item capacity - Open, Nearly full, Full or Overbooked
  • Bake-day totals: quantity of every item for up to four pickup days, plus batches to bake
  • Batch Scaler calculator: scale five ingredients to the number of items you need
  • Estimated ingredient cost and gross profit per item
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, items by product, items by slot and sales by category
  • Dropdowns for slots, items and payment methods - edit them on their tabs

What you get

  • Holiday Pre-Order, Pickup Slot & Bake-Day Planner (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
