Home Renovation Budget & Contractor Bid Tracker

Keep your renovation on budget: compare contractor bids, log every payment and track the punch list in one workbook.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Renovations go over budget one small surprise at a time. A quote here, a change order there, a deposit you forgot to write down, and suddenly nobody knows what's left. This workbook keeps the whole project in one place so you can see where the money stands before you sign the next check.

Set a budget for each line with a contingency cushion, put up to three contractor bids side by side, and log every deposit, draw and materials run against the line it belongs to. Approved change orders adjust the budget automatically, each line shows On track, Near limit, Into contingency or Over budget, and the punch list flags snags that are due or overdue. The dashboard shows total budget, spent, remaining, savings from comparing bids and what still needs a decision. It is a planning and record-keeping tool; it does not give financial or building advice.

What it does

  • Budget lines by room and category with an adjustable contingency %
  • Status per line: Not started, On track, Near limit, Into contingency, Over budget
  • Bid comparison: three contractors side by side, lowest bid, spread and savings vs the highest quote
  • Payments log for deposits, progress draws, materials and permits, with a receipt check
  • Change order log: approved costs flow into the budget, slow decisions turn red
  • Punch list with due dates and Open / Due soon / Overdue / Done status
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly spending chart, budget vs spent by category, spending by payment type

What you get

  • Home Renovation Budget & Contractor Bid Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
