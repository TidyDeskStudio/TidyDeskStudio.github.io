Small Nonprofit Donor, Gift & Thank-You Tracker

One place for donors, gifts, campaign goals and who still needs a thank-you.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Giving season is busy, and a small team can't afford to lose track of a gift or forget to say thank you. Donor software is often too much for a club or community group, and a plain list doesn't tell you much.

This tracker logs every gift against a donor and campaign, flags gifts that still need a thank-you (and ones that are overdue), and totals each supporter's lifetime, this-year and last-year giving so you can see who gave last year but hasn't yet this year. Campaigns show progress against goal, and the dashboard shows money raised by month. It is a record-keeping tool only: it doesn't create tax receipts or give tax advice.

What it does

  • Gift log with To thank, Overdue thank-you and Thanked status, plus days taken to thank
  • Donor list with lifetime, this-year and last-year giving and last gift date
  • Donor status: Active, Gave last year, Lapsed or Prospect
  • Campaigns with goals, % of goal and Running, Upcoming, Ended, Ongoing or Goal met
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, money raised by month, raised by campaign and by method
  • Gift types for one-time, monthly, pledge, in-kind and event gifts
  • Set your own thank-you window, currency and dashboard year

What you get

  • Small Nonprofit Donor, Gift & Thank-You Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
