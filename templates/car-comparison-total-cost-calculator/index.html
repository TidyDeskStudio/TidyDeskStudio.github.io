Car Shopping Comparison & Total Cost of Ownership Calculator

Compare every car on your shortlist by its real cost over the years you'll own it - loan, fuel, insurance, upkeep and resale included.

Get it on Gumroad

The cheapest car on the forecourt is not always the cheapest car to own. A lower price can hide a higher interest rate, thirstier fuel use, pricier insurance or a weaker resale value, and it is hard to weigh all of that in your head.

This calculator puts your shortlist side by side. Enter each car's price, loan terms, fuel economy and yearly costs once. It works out the monthly payment, total interest, running costs, total cost of ownership after expected resale, cost per year and cost per mile, ranks the cars and flags anything over your budget. A test-drive scorecard adds how each car actually felt. All figures are estimates from your own inputs - a comparison tool, not financial advice.

What it does

  • Side-by-side comparison of up to 40 cars - new, nearly new or used
  • Loan maths: monthly payment and total interest (or leave the loan blank for cash)
  • Fuel or charging cost per year from your mileage, fuel economy and editable fuel prices
  • Total cost of ownership, cost per year and cost per mile, after expected resale value
  • Automatic ranking plus Lowest cost, Strong pick, Consider and Over budget verdicts
  • Test-drive scorecard (comfort, space, driving feel, tech, visibility) averaged per car
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, cheapest-cars leaderboard, budget-health ring, total cost and yearly running-cost charts by car, cars-by-verdict chart, cost per year chart, shortlist checks and an over-budget watch list

What you get

  • Car Cost Comparison (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
