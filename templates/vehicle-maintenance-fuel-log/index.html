Vehicle Maintenance & Fuel Log

Fuel, services and maintenance reminders for every car you own - with real cost per mile or km.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Cars cost more than the fuel pump suggests, and service intervals are easy to forget until a warning light comes on. This log keeps fuel, services and reminders for every vehicle in one workbook.

Log fill-ups and services as they happen. Set reminders by distance, months or both, and each one shows when it's due and whether it's Overdue, Due soon or OK. Every vehicle shows its fuel and service spend for the year and its cost per mile or km, and the dashboard compares your cars month by month.

What it does

  • Fuel log with automatic price per litre, gallon or kWh
  • Service and repair log per vehicle
  • Reminders by distance and/or months with Overdue / Due soon / OK status
  • Per-vehicle fuel spend, service spend, distance and cost per mile or km
  • Works for several vehicles - petrol, diesel or electric
  • Dashboard: fuel and service spend, total running cost, average fuel price, reminders due
  • Charts: fuel vs services by month, costs by vehicle, service spend by type

What you get

  • Vehicle Maintenance & Fuel Log (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
