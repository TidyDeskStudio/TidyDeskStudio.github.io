Mobile Car Detailing Booking & Job Profit Tracker

Log detailing bookings by package and vehicle size and see which packages actually make you money.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Prices that change with vehicle size, supplies that disappear faster than you think and regulars who drift away if you don't message them in time - running a detailing business means juggling a lot in your head.

This tracker turns your service menu into a price grid, so every booking prices itself from the package and vehicle size. Mark jobs done and paid, add tips and hours, and it works out profit and profit per hour after supplies and travel. Each client's last detail shows when they're due to rebook, and the dashboard shows revenue, tips, no-shows and profit by package.

What it does

  • Package menu with a price for small cars, SUVs and large vehicles / vans
  • Booking log that fills in the package price, plus add-ons, discounts and tips
  • Supply cost per package and travel cost per job taken off for real profit
  • Profit and profit per hour on every finished job
  • Alerts for unpaid jobs, no-shows, upcoming bookings and past bookings to update
  • Rebook now / Rebook soon reminders from each client's preferred interval
  • Client list with details done, total spent, tips and no-shows
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly revenue vs profit, profit by package, revenue by vehicle size
