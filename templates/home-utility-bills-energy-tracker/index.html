Home Utility Bills & Energy Usage Tracker

See exactly where your power, gas and water money goes - usage, cost per unit and this year vs last year.

Get it on Payhip

The heating comes on, the bill jumps, and you are left wondering whether you used more or just paid more. Bills arrive at different times and in different units, so comparing them is hard.

This tracker puts every utility bill in one list. It works out cost per unit and cost per day, finds the same month last year and flags any bill that is up by more than the percentage you choose, or where you used noticeably more than a year ago. Due dates turn into Due soon and Overdue reminders, and an optional meter readings tab shows daily usage between bills and catches readings that don't add up.

What it does

  • Bills log for any utility: electricity, gas, water, heating oil and more
  • Automatic cost per unit, cost per day and change vs the same month last year
  • Alerts when a bill costs more, or uses more, than the same month last year - you set the %
  • Payment status: Paid, Unpaid, Due soon and Overdue
  • Utilities tab with this year vs last year cost, usage, average bill and a budget check
  • Meter readings tab with usage per day and a check for readings that go backwards
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly cost chart and this year vs last year by utility

What you get

  • Home Utility Bills & Energy Usage Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
