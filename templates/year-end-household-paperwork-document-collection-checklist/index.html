Every year the same thing happens: a pile of letters, a few emails, and no clear idea which statements have actually arrived. Without one list, missing forms only show up when you sit down to file.

This free spreadsheet keeps one row per document you expect. Each row shows Received, Waiting, Due soon, Chase now or Not needed, plus a next step such as Contact source or Save a copy. The Sources tab shows how many documents each employer, bank or office still owes you, and a preparation steps list keeps the jobs moving. The dashboard adds an expected-by calendar, a person spotlight, a percent-received ring, alerts and charts. It is a record-keeping tool only and not tax, legal or financial advice.

What it does

  • Documents tab with category, person, source, expected date and status dropdowns
  • Automatic flag on every document: Received, Waiting, Due soon, Chase now, No date or Not needed
  • Next step column such as Contact source, Watch for it or Save a copy
  • Sources tab showing documents, received, still missing and percent received for each source
  • Preparation steps list with owners, due dates and Overdue or Due soon flags
  • Dashboard with an expected-by calendar, person spotlight dropdown, tick-box preparation steps and attention lists
  • Percent-received ring and a days-to-filing-target counter
  • Charts for documents received by month, by category and by person, with sample data and a Quick Start Guide
