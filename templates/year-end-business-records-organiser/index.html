Year-End Business Records Organiser for Sole Traders

Gather every invoice, expense, receipt and document in one place and hand your accountant a clean summary.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Year-end is when the shoebox appears: invoices in one folder, receipts in another, a bank statement you still need to download, and a question from your accountant you cannot answer. Sorting it all out in December is a lot easier if it has been tracked in one place.

This spreadsheet gives you an income log, an expense log with receipt checks, a category summary, a document checklist and a one-page Accountant Summary. The dashboard shows what is still missing: receipts to find, documents to chase and invoices not yet paid. It is a record-keeping tool only. It does not give tax, legal or accounting advice.

What it does

  • Dashboard with biggest expenses, a documents-collected ring, progress bars and three watch lists
  • Income log with invoice references, paid or unpaid status and an invoice-on-file check
  • Expense log with business-use percentage, paid-with method and automatic receipt check
  • Category tab with totals, business amounts, missing receipts and share of spending
  • Document checklist grouped by income, expenses, bank, equipment and admin
  • Accountant Summary page with income, spending, gaps and counts ready to send
  • Monthly income vs spending chart and a spending-by-category heatmap
  • Dropdowns, sample data and a Quick Start Guide included

What you get

  • Year-End Business Records Organiser for Sole Traders (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
