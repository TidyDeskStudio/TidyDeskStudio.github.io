House Hunting Comparison & Viewing Scorecard

After ten viewings they all blur together. Score every home on what matters to you and compare them fairly.

Get it on Gumroad

House hunting gets confusing fast. By the fifth viewing you are mixing up kitchens, forgetting which one had the damp and arguing about gut feelings. A scorecard keeps the decision about what you actually decided matters.

Set your weights once, then score each home from 1 to 10 straight after the viewing. The spreadsheet turns that into a fit score out of 100, estimates the monthly cost from your own deposit, rate and budget, flags homes that are over budget or fail a must-have, and shows your top three side by side. The monthly figures are estimates for comparing homes, not mortgage, legal or financial advice.

What it does

  • Dashboard with a Top 5 leaderboard, search-progress bars, a watch list and a viewings heatmap
  • Criteria tab where you weight what matters, from nice to have to essential
  • Viewing log with price, bedrooms, bathrooms, fees, notes and six scores per home
  • Fit score out of 100 and rank for every home, weighted by your own priorities
  • Estimated monthly cost using your deposit, interest rate, loan length, tax, insurance and upkeep
  • Budget check: Within budget, Near limit or Over budget against your monthly maximum
  • Verdicts: Top pick, Worth a second look, Low fit, Over budget or Dealbreaker
  • Top 3 tab, a questions-to-ask checklist with a completion ring, and a pipeline view by home status

What you get

  • House Hunting Comparison & Viewing Scorecard (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
