Holiday Gift Budget & Shopping Tracker

Keep holiday spending on track - a budget per person, every gift from idea to wrapped.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Holiday shopping gets expensive when it lives in your head and a dozen browser tabs. This tracker keeps the whole list in one place: who you're buying for, what you planned to spend, what you actually paid and what's still left to do.

Set a budget for each person, add gift ideas with an estimate, and update the status as you shop. Every person shows planned, spent and what's left, with a flag the moment someone goes over. The dashboard shows your total against budget and how many gifts still need buying or wrapping.

What it does

  • Budget per person with planned, spent and left - plus an Over budget flag
  • Gift list with estimate vs actual cost and over/under
  • Status for every item: Idea, Ordered, Bought, Wrapped, Given - with a clear To do column
  • Room for cards, food, wrapping and decorations as well as gifts
  • Last Year tab so nobody gets the same gift twice
  • Dashboard: total budget, planned, spent, left to allocate, still to buy, ready to give
  • Charts: budget vs planned per person, spend by category, list progress

What you get

  • Holiday Gift Budget & Shopping Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
