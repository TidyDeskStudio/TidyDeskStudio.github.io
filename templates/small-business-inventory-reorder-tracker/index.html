Small Business Inventory & Reorder Tracker

See your stock levels, get a reorder list before things run out, and spot slow sellers - in one spreadsheet.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Running out of your best sellers while slow stock gathers dust is a cash problem, and it usually happens because nobody knows the real stock count. This tracker gives every item a live stock level from a simple log of deliveries, sales, returns and write-offs.

Set a reorder point per item and the sheet tells you what to order now, what to order soon and roughly what it will cost, supplier by supplier. Items with no sales in your chosen window are marked as slow movers, and the dashboard shows stock value, sales and purchases for the year.

What it does

  • Live stock level per item from a stock-in / sale / return / write-off / count log
  • Statuses: OK, Reorder soon, Reorder now, Out of stock, Slow mover
  • Suggested order quantity and order cost for every item that needs restocking
  • Days of stock left based on recent sales, plus supplier lead time on the same row
  • Supplier tab: items, stock value, items to reorder, order cost and minimum-order check
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, units sold vs received by month, stock value and sales by category
  • Adjustable reorder buffer and slow-mover window, any currency symbol

What you get

  • Small Business Inventory & Reorder Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
