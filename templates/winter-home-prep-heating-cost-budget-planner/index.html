Winter Home Prep & Heating Cost Budget Planner

Plan winter prep jobs, log energy bills and see every month of heating cost against budget so December bills do not surprise you.

Get it on Gumroad

Winter has a habit of arriving before the boiler is serviced, the pipes are lagged or the heating budget is worked out. Then the first big bill lands and nobody knows where the money went.

This spreadsheet gives you a winter prep job list with owners, due dates, estimated and actual costs, an energy bill log by supplier and fuel, and a month-by-month heating budget with an Under, On budget, Over or Planned status. A winter kit tab flags what to restock, and the dashboard pulls it together with a job calendar, tick-box weekly routine, alerts and charts. It is a household record-keeping tool and not energy, safety or financial advice.

What it does

  • Winter prep job list with area, owner, priority, due date, status, estimated and actual cost
  • Days left and a Done, On track, Due soon or Overdue flag for every job
  • Energy bill log by supplier and fuel that feeds the monthly heating budget
  • Heating budget vs actual for each month with an Under, On budget, Over or Planned status
  • Winter kit tab that flags Low and Out items as a restock list
  • Dashboard with month and helper dropdowns, a job calendar and a tick-box weekly routine
  • Charts for spend by month and fuel, a fuel-by-month heatmap and ranked bills
  • Sample data, clickable navigation and a Quick Start Guide included

What you get

  • Winter Home Prep & Heating Cost Budget Planner (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)
