Household Warranty, Receipt & Big-Purchase Tracker

Stop hunting for the receipt. Log every big purchase once and see what is still covered.

Get it on Gumroad

The dishwasher stops working, and suddenly you need a purchase date, a serial number and a receipt you last saw in spring. Most households keep this information in five different places, or nowhere at all.

This spreadsheet keeps it in one. Add each big purchase with its warranty length and return window, and the sheet works out the end dates, counts the days left, flags return windows that are still open and reminds you about receipts you cannot find. A Claims tab tracks repairs and refunds. It is a record-keeping tool; always check the seller's own warranty terms.

What it does

  • Dashboard with a needs-action list, receipt ring, biggest purchases and a yearly budget bar
  • Warranty end date and days-left countdown worked out for every purchase
  • Colour-coded status: Covered, Expiring soon, Expired, Returned or Passed on
  • Return deadline and an Open or Closed return-window flag
  • Fields for model number, serial number, store and where the receipt is kept
  • Paper trail check that flags every purchase with no receipt you can find
  • Claims tab with status, amount covered and days a claim has been open
  • Spending by month, category and store, plus a category-by-month heatmap

  • Household Warranty, Receipt & Big-Purchase Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
