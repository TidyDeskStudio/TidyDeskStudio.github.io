Year-End Physical Inventory Count & Stock Valuation

Count by location, compare with your records, flag what to recount and see what your stock is worth at cost.

Get it on Gumroad

Year-end is coming, the stockroom is a guess and your records say one thing while the shelves say another. A paper count sheet gets you numbers but not the difference, the value or the list of items you need to check again.

This spreadsheet gives you a count sheet with the recorded quantity next to the counted quantity for every item. It works out the variance in units, percent and value at cost, flags Match, Over, Short or Recount, takes damaged units out of the sellable stock value and shows progress by location. A shrinkage check compares the count with what your purchases and sales say you should hold. It is a record-keeping tool, not accounting or tax advice.

What it does

  • Count sheet with recorded quantity, counted quantity and damaged units for every item
  • Variance in units, percent and value at cost on each row
  • Status per item: Not counted, Match, Over, Short or Recount using tolerances you set
  • Sellable stock value and damaged write-off value at cost
  • Locations tab showing items listed, items counted and Not started, In progress or Done
  • Adjustments log with reasons and loss value
  • Shrinkage Check tab comparing expected and counted stock against your limit
  • Dashboard with biggest variances, count progress, category chart and recount list

What you get

  • Year-End Physical Inventory Count & Stock Valuation (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
