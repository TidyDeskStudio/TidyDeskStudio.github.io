Pet Sitter & Dog Walker Visit & Payment Tracker

Log every walk and pet visit, keep client and pet details handy, and see who still owes you.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

When you're out walking dogs and feeding cats all day, the admin piles up: which visits are paid, who still owes for last week, where the spare key is kept, what the vet's name is. This tracker keeps all of it in one workbook.

Log a visit in one row and the price fills in from your own price list. Mark it paid when the money arrives. Future bookings show as Booked, unpaid visits turn red once they pass your payment window, and a monthly statement tab totals any client's month in seconds. The dashboard shows what you've charged and received, what's owed, and which services and clients keep you busiest.

What it does

  • Visit log with check-in and check-out times, visit notes and a Week of column for planning
  • Prices fill in from your own Services list, plus an Extra column for second pets or holiday rates
  • Colour-coded status: Paid, Unpaid, Overdue, Booked
  • Client and pet records with care notes, access notes, emergency contact and vet
  • Client Statement tab: visits, charges and amount due for any client and month
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, charged vs received by month, revenue by service, owed by client
  • Any currency symbol and your own overdue window

What you get

  • Pet Sitter & Dog Walker Visit & Payment Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
