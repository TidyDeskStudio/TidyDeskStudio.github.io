Small Business Expense & Receipt Log

Log every business expense, catch missing receipts and see where your money goes each month.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

When it's time to sort out the books, the hard part is rarely the maths. It's remembering what that card charge in March was, finding the receipt, and working out how much you spent on software versus travel. Doing it once a year means hours of digging.

This log gives every expense one row: date, vendor, category, how you paid, the amount and any tax shown on the receipt. It flags rows that still need a receipt and turns them red once your deadline passes, tracks purchases you paid for personally until you pay yourself back, and compares each category with a monthly budget. The dashboard shows this year and this month, average spend per month, tax recorded, missing receipts and charts by month, category and payment method. It is a record-keeping tool only, not tax or accounting advice.

What it does

  • Expense log with 13 editable categories and payment-method dropdowns
  • Receipt tracking: Filed, Get receipt, or Receipt missing after your own deadline
  • Receipt reference column for the folder or file where each receipt lives
  • Paid personally? Shows Reimburse me until you record the pay-back date
  • Tax-included column with net amount worked out for you
  • Category budgets: monthly amounts compared with spending to date (On track, Near limit, Over budget)
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly spending chart, spending by category and by payment method

What you get

  • Small Business Expense & Receipt Log (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
