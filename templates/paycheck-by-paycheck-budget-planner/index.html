You're running OUT of money before payday and can't tell where it went.

Paycheck-by-Paycheck Budget Planner with Bill Assignment

Enter your bills once and see which paycheck covers each one, what is left over and which paychecks fall short.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

When you are paid every week or every two weeks, a monthly budget does not match real life. The rent lands on one paycheck, the car payment and the insurance on the next, and suddenly one paycheck is empty before payday.

This spreadsheet builds your paycheck calendar from your first payday, then assigns each monthly bill to the paycheck that is current on its due day. It subtracts the bills, your fund savings and your planned spending, and shows what is left on every paycheck with a status of On track, Tight or Short. It is a planning tool for your own records, not financial advice.

What it does

  • Dashboard with biggest bills, a fund-progress ring, goal bars and a short-paycheck watch list
  • Paycheck calendar for weekly or every-two-weeks pay, up to 52 paychecks
  • Bills assigned to a paycheck automatically from their due day
  • Leftover and status (On track, Tight or Short) for every paycheck
  • Bill list with category, due day, autopay flag, share of bills and yearly cost
  • Sinking funds with progress, paychecks needed and a Behind or On track check
  • Pay vs bills and leftover charts by month
  • Dropdowns, sample data and a Quick Start Guide included

What you get

  • Paycheck-by-Paycheck Budget Planner with Bill Assignment (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
