Reseller Inventory & Profit Tracker

See your real profit on every flip - after fees, shipping and expenses.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Reselling feels profitable until you add up fees, shipping, supplies and the stuff that's been sitting on a shelf for months. This tracker follows every item from the day you buy it to the day it sells and tells you exactly what you made.

Log an item when you source it, update it when you list it and when it sells. Profit, ROI, days to sell and days in stock work themselves out, and the dashboard shows your best categories and channels, plus how much cash is tied up in unsold stock.

What it does

  • Per-item profit after fees and shipping, ROI % and days to sell
  • Status for every item: Not listed, Listed, Sold - plus days in stock for slow movers
  • Running-expenses log so your profit figure is the real one
  • Dashboard: sales, profit, profit after expenses, money tied up in stock, average ROI and days to sell
  • Charts: monthly sales vs profit vs expenses, profit by category, results by selling channel
  • Rename channels, sources and categories to match the platforms you sell on
  • Room for 1,000 items (copy rows down for more)

What you get

  • Reseller Inventory & Profit Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
