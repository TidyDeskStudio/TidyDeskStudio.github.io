Home Move Planner, Checklist & Budget

Plan your move week by week: a dated checklist, box inventory, mover quotes, address changes and a moving budget.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Moving means a hundred small jobs with deadlines, and the hardest part is remembering them all while you pack. This planner starts from one date, your move day, and works out when every task is due, so you can see what's overdue and what's coming up this week.

Number your boxes by room and mark the ones you'll need first. Compare mover quotes side by side, budget each moving cost against what you actually pay, and tick off every bank, employer, doctor and utility that needs your new address. The dashboard counts down the days and shows packing progress and spending.

What it does

  • 16-task starter checklist with due dates that count back from your move date
  • Statuses: Done, Overdue, This week, Upcoming - change the move date and everything updates
  • Box inventory by room with fragile flag and unpack priority (Open first, First week, Storage)
  • Mover quote comparison that marks the cheapest option free on your date
  • Moving cost budget vs actual with over-budget warnings
  • Address-change list so nobody is forgotten
  • Dashboard: days to go, % done, overdue tasks, boxes packed, spending, plus 3 charts

What you get

  • Home Move Planner, Checklist & Budget (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
