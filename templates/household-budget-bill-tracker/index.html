Household Budget & Bill Tracker

A calm, simple monthly budget: categories, bills with due dates, and a clear view of where your money goes.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

No app, no subscription, no bank login - just a clean workbook that shows where your money goes. Set a budget for each category, add your bills with the day they're due, and log your income and spending.

Pick any month and the budget tab shows what you've spent, what's left and which categories are over. The bills tab works out each bill's next due date and warns you three days ahead. The dashboard gives you the month at a glance and the whole year by month.

What it does

  • Monthly budget by category with spent, left, % used and OK / Close / Over status
  • Switch the budget month with one cell to review any month
  • Bills list with automatic next due date, days left and Due soon / Autopay alerts
  • Transaction log for income and spending with categories and accounts
  • Dashboard: income, spending, budget left, categories over budget, year totals, savings, bills due
  • Charts: income vs spending by month, budget vs spent, spending by category
  • Rename or add your own categories

What you get

  • Household Budget & Bill Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
