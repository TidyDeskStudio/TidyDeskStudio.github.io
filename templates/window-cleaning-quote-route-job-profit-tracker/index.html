Window Cleaning Quote, Route & Job Profit Tracker

Quote by pane count, keep repeat rounds on schedule and see the real profit and hourly rate of every clean.

Get it on Gumroad

You price by the pane, run the same streets every few weeks and carry most of it in your head. Then a regular is two weeks overdue, a big job turns out to pay less per hour than the small ones, and nobody has chased the invoice.

This spreadsheet gives every customer a quote price based on panes and storeys, tells you who is due or overdue for their next clean, and logs each job with hours, miles, supplies and helper pay. You see profit, profit per hour and profit per pane on every clean, flagged Low rate, OK or Strong, plus what each route day is worth and who still owes you money. It is a record-keeping and pricing tool, not accounting or tax advice.

What it does

  • Customer list with automatic quote price from panes, storeys, your price per pane and a minimum charge
  • Repeat-clean schedule: last clean, next due date and Overdue, Due soon or On track flags
  • Job log with billed amount, costs, profit, margin, profit per hour and per pane
  • Low rate, OK and Strong flags using hourly limits you set
  • Payment status per job: Scheduled, To be paid, Overdue, Paid
  • Pane Quote tab for new enquiries with an estimated profit per hour and a verdict
  • Route Day tab showing customers, panes, round value and customers due for any weekday
  • Dashboard with best-job leaderboard, goal bars, area heatmap and money-to-chase list

What you get

  • Window Cleaning Quote, Route & Job Profit Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
