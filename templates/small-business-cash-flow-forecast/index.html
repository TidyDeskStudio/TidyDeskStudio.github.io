12-Month Small Business Cash Flow Forecast

See which months will be tight before they arrive - a simple 12-month cash flow forecast for small businesses.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

You know roughly what comes in and what goes out, but not which month the bank balance will dip. Rent, wages, a yearly insurance bill and a quiet season can land at the same time, and you find out too late.

This workbook keeps the plan simple: one row per amount you expect, in or out. It adds everything up month by month, works out your projected bank balance from your opening cash, and marks any month that falls below your comfort level or below zero. As each month happens, type the real figures and the forecast updates around them. It is a planning and record-keeping tool, not financial advice.

What it does

  • Simple plan list: one row per expected amount, money in or money out, with 12 editable categories
  • Forecast vs actual on every line, with On track, Short, Ahead and Over budget checks
  • Month-by-month totals for the year in your settings - change one number to plan next year
  • Projected bank balance for every month, using actual figures where you have them
  • Low cash and Overdrawn warnings based on a comfort level you choose
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, money in vs out chart, projected balance line and spending by category
  • Any currency symbol and an adjustable tolerance for flagging differences

What you get

  • 12-Month Small Business Cash Flow Forecast (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
