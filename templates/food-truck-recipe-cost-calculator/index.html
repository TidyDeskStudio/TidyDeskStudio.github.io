Food Truck & Small Kitchen Recipe Cost Calculator

Know the real cost of every plate - and when a price rise means it's time to re-price.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Ingredient prices move every few weeks, and it's easy to keep selling a dish that quietly stopped making money. This calculator costs every plate from your actual pack prices and shows which items need attention.

Enter your ingredients once with pack price and size (kg, g, litres, ml or each). Build each menu item from its ingredients per plate. The sheet works out plate cost, food-cost %, margin and the menu price that hits your target, and flags anything over target. When a supplier raises a price, change it once and every plate updates.

What it does

  • Ingredient price list with automatic cost per gram, ml or item
  • Recipe lines per menu item, costs looked up for you
  • Plate cost, food-cost %, margin per plate and price at your target %
  • Good / Watch / Raise price check on every item
  • Price Check calculator to test a supplier price rise
  • Dashboard: average and highest food-cost %, items to re-price, average margin
  • Charts: food-cost % by item, margin by category

What you get

  • Food Truck & Small Kitchen Recipe Cost Calculator (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
