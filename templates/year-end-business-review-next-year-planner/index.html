Every December the same scramble starts: what did we actually sell this year, which months and clients carried us, did we make a profit, and what should next year's targets be?

This spreadsheet adds up the year from a simple Sales tab and an Expenses tab, shows revenue, direct costs, expenses and profit month by month, flags your Best month, Weakest month and any Loss month, and ranks your clients with a Key client warning when one client is too large a share. A year-end checklist tracks every record to gather with Overdue and Due soon flags, and a Goals tab turns this year's quarters into next year's targets with the growth you need. The dashboard adds a calendar, quarter spotlight, alerts and charts. It is a record-keeping and planning tool and not tax, accounting or financial advice.

What it does

  • Sales tab with customer and product dropdowns, amount, direct cost and gross profit
  • Expenses tab with categories and a month label
  • Month by month tab with revenue, costs, profit, margin and Best, Weakest or Loss month flags
  • Clients tab with revenue, orders, share of the year and a Key client or Inactive tier
  • Next-year plan by quarter with revenue target, expense cap and the growth needed over this year
  • Year-end checklist with owners, due dates and Overdue, Due soon, To do or Done flags
  • Dashboard with month and quarter dropdowns, a tasks calendar and a tick-box records checklist
  • Alerts plus charts for revenue vs expenses, revenue by product and expenses by category, with sample data and a Quick Start Guide
