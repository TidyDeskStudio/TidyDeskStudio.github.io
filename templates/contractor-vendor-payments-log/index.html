Contractor & Vendor Payments Log with Year-End Totals

Log every contractor and vendor payment and have each payee's yearly total ready when you need it.

Get it on Payhip

Come year end, many small business owners end up scrolling through bank statements to work out how much they paid each freelancer or subcontractor, and whether they ever got a contract or invoice from them.

This log keeps every payment in one place. Each payee's total for this year, last year and all time is worked out for you, payments without an invoice are flagged, and payees missing a contract or details on file are highlighted. You can set your own review amount to keep an eye on bigger yearly totals. It is a record-keeping tool only - not tax or legal advice, and it doesn't fill in any forms.

What it does

  • Payments log with fees, reimbursed expenses, total paid and quarter
  • Invoice received check - payments without an invoice are flagged
  • Payees tab with payments and totals for this year, last year and all time
  • Paperwork status per payee: Complete, Missing paperwork or Review (at or above your own review amount)
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly totals, and totals by category and by payee
  • Change the year to total any year in your records
  • Dropdowns for payees (edit on the Payees tab) and for categories and payment methods (edit on the Lists tab)

What you get

  • Contractor & Vendor Payments Log with Year-End Totals (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
