Pressure Washing Quote & Job Profit Tracker

Quote by the square foot, track every quote and see what each pressure washing job really earns.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Guessing prices on the driveway, losing track of quotes you sent last week and not knowing which jobs pay once chemicals, fuel and your time come off - it adds up.

This workbook keeps your rates, quotes, jobs and customers together. Set a price per square foot and minimum charge for each surface and every quote prices itself. Log finished jobs with costs and hours to see profit, margin and earnings per hour, track who still owes you, and get a reminder when regular customers are due their next clean. A Quick Quote calculator prices a job on the spot.

What it does

  • Rates tab: price per square foot and minimum charge for each surface you clean
  • Quote log that prices itself, with Sent / Won / Lost status and a Follow up alert
  • Job log with chemicals, fuel, other costs and labour - profit, margin and earnings per hour per job
  • Payment tracking: Paid, Part paid and Unpaid with the balance owed
  • Repeat-service reminders from each client's cleaning interval: Rebook now, Due soon
  • Quick Quote calculator for pricing on site
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly revenue vs profit, profit by surface, top clients and quotes by status

What you get

  • Pressure Washing Quote & Job Profit Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
