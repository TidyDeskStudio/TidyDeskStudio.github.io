Net Worth & Account Balance Tracker

See every account and debt in one place and track your net worth month by month.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

When your money is spread across a checking account, savings, a retirement plan and a couple of debts, it's hard to tell if you're actually moving forward. This tracker brings it together with one quick update a month.

List your accounts and debts once, then add each balance on the 1st of the month. The workbook works out your net worth, total assets and debts, what changed since last month and since January, and charts how much it changed each month. Set goals like an emergency fund target or paying a card to zero and see how far is left. It's a record-keeping tool for your own numbers, not financial advice.

What it does

  • Accounts list for assets and debts with type, owner and where it's held
  • Monthly balance log - debts typed as positive numbers, subtracted for you
  • Latest balance, previous balance, change and Better / Worse trend for every account
  • Goals tab for saving up or paying down, with Reached / In progress / Missed date
  • Dashboard: net worth, assets, debts, change since last update and since January, debts as % of assets
  • Monthly chart of how much your net worth changed, split into asset growth and debt paid down, plus asset mix by type
  • Any currency symbol

What you get

  • Net Worth & Account Balance Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros. A record-keeping tool, not financial advice.
