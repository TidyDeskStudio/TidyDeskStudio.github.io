Craft Fair & Market Vendor Event Profit Tracker

Find out which craft fairs and markets are really worth the booth fee - and what stock to bring next time.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

A busy table doesn't always mean a good day. Once you take off the booth fee, fuel, parking, display costs and what your stock cost to make, some events barely pay - and it is hard to remember which ones when the next application deadline comes round.

This tracker keeps every fair, market and pop-up in one place. Plan ahead with apply-by dates and application status, then log your takings after the day. It works out net profit, return on fees, profit per hour, visitor conversion and average sale, gives each event a verdict, and ranks your events on the dashboard. An optional stock count shows sell-through per item so you pack smarter. It is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice.

What it does

  • Event calendar with apply-by dates and a Next step column: Apply soon, Missed, Coming up, Booked, Log sales
  • Cash and card takings, booth fee, extra costs and stock cost combined into net profit per event
  • Return on fees, profit per hour, visitor conversion and average sale for every event
  • Verdict per event - Worth it, Marginal or Lost money - based on your own target hourly profit
  • Stock Count tab: brought vs returned, sell-through and Bring more / Bring less hints
  • Costs tab for travel, parking, accommodation, display and card fees, linked to each event
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly sales vs profit chart, event ranking, results by event type, costs by category
  • Editable dropdowns for event types, application status and cost categories

What you get

  • Craft Fair & Market Vendor Event Profit Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
