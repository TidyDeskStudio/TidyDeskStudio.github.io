Freelancer Invoice & Payment Tracker

Track every invoice, spot late payers and see your real income - in Excel or Google Sheets.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Stop digging through emails to work out who still owes you. This tracker keeps every invoice in one place and does the maths for you: due dates, tax, balances and overdue alerts update the moment you type.

Add an invoice in one row. Mark it paid when the money lands. The dashboard shows what you've invoiced and received this year, how much is overdue, how long clients take to pay, and which services and clients bring in the most.

What it does

  • Automatic due dates from your payment terms (per client or default)
  • Colour-coded status: Paid, Open, Due soon, Overdue - plus days late
  • Partial payments handled - balance shows exactly what's left
  • Client list with invoiced, paid and outstanding totals
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly invoiced vs received chart, income by service, top clients
  • Optional tax / VAT rate, any currency symbol
  • Dropdowns for clients, services and payment methods - edit them on the Lists tab

What you get

  • Freelancer Invoice & Payment Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
