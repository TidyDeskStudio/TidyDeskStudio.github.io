Mobile Mechanic Job, Parts & Invoice Tracker

Track every mobile repair job - vehicle, labor, parts, price and payment - in one simple workbook.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

When you work out of a van, the paperwork ends up on receipts in the glovebox and notes on your phone. Did you charge for that caliper? Has the customer from two weeks ago paid yet? What did you actually make last month after parts?

This tracker gives every job one row. Pick the customer and the vehicle fills in, enter your labor hours and call-out fee, and log each part against the job number. Your labor rate and parts markup turn that into a job total and profit, the status shows Booked, In progress, Waiting parts, Unpaid, Overdue or Paid, and the Invoice tab pulls up a clean summary of any job to send to the customer. The dashboard shows billed, received, profit after parts, parts spend and who still owes you.

What it does

  • Jobs log with stages: Booked, In progress, Waiting parts, Done
  • Automatic status: Paid, Unpaid or Overdue after your payment terms
  • Parts log linked to job numbers with cost, markup (default or per part) and price charged
  • Labor worked out from hours and your hourly rate, plus an optional call-out fee
  • Customers & Vehicles tab with plate, mileage, jobs, total billed, amount owed and last job date
  • Invoice tab: type a job number for a printable job summary with balance due and pay-by date
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly billed / profit / parts chart, income by job type, top customers

What you get

  • Mobile Mechanic Job, Parts & Invoice Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
