Wedding Budget & Guest List Planner

One planner for the money and the guests: budget, supplier payments, RSVPs and meals.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Wedding planning is two big spreadsheets in disguise: what everything costs, and who's coming. This planner does both and links them to one dashboard.

Set your total budget, estimate each part of the day, then swap in the real cost when you book. You'll always know what's left to allocate, what you've paid, what's still owed and which supplier payments are due in the next 30 days. The guest list tracks RSVPs, meals, plus-ones, tables, gifts and thank-you notes, with an automatic headcount.

What it does

  • Budget by category with estimate, actual cost and over/under
  • Supplier payments: deposit, other payments and balance due date
  • Payment status: Estimate, Booked, Due soon (30 days), Overdue, Paid
  • Guest list with side, group, RSVP, meal, plus-one, table, gift and thank-you tracking
  • Automatic headcount including plus-ones
  • Dashboard: budget left, paid so far, still owed, due in 30 days, guests attending, countdown to the day
  • Charts: estimate vs actual by category, RSVP status, meal choices

What you get

  • Wedding Budget & Guest List Planner (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
