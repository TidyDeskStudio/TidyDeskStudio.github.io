Subscriptions have a way of piling up quietly. A streaming plan here, a club there, a yearly renewal nobody remembers signing up for - and no single place that shows what they all add up to.

This free spreadsheet gives every subscription and recurring bill one row. Enter the name, category, who pays, how often it bills, the cost and the next renewal date, then pick Keep, Review or Cancel. The file works out the cost per month and per year, counts the days left, flags renewals that are coming up, and compares each category with the budget you set. The dashboard adds a renewal calendar, a weekly review checklist, charts and a cancel-or-review list. It is a household record-keeping tool and not financial advice.

What it does

  • Free download: one row per subscription or recurring bill, with cost per month and per year worked out for monthly, quarterly and yearly bills
  • Renewal calendar on the dashboard that shows the money leaving on each day of the month you pick

  • Keep, Review or Cancel decision column with a review and cancel list and the yearly saving if you cancel
  • Category budgets with Within budget, Near limit and Over budget flags
  • Owner dropdown and spotlight cards to see who pays for what
  • Weekly review checklist with tick boxes, alerts, awards and a monthly budget ring
  • Sample data, dropdowns, clickable navigation and a Quick Start Guide included
