Freelance Client CRM & Sales Pipeline

A simple CRM for freelancers: leads, follow-ups, weighted pipeline and win rate - no subscription.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Most freelancers lose work not because they're bad at it, but because a promising lead didn't get a follow-up. This spreadsheet CRM keeps every lead in one place and tells you who to contact next.

Set your stages and win probabilities once. Each lead shows its weighted value and a follow-up flag - Today, Overdue, Going cold or Scheduled. The dashboard shows open pipeline, weighted forecast, business won this year, win rate and which lead sources actually turn into paying clients.

What it does

  • Lead list with source, service, estimated value, stage, last contact and next follow-up
  • Customisable stages with win probabilities and weighted pipeline value
  • Follow-up alerts: Today, Overdue, Going cold (you set the days), Scheduled
  • Won / lost tracking with close dates
  • Dashboard: open pipeline, weighted forecast, won this year, win rate, follow-ups due, average deal
  • Charts: won business by month, pipeline by stage, lead sources
  • No subscription, no logins - works offline

What you get

  • Freelance Client CRM & Sales Pipeline (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
