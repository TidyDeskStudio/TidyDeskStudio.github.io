Cleaning Business Job & Client Tracker

Run your cleaning business from one sheet - jobs, clients, unpaid invoices and real profit.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Built for cleaners who are busy cleaning, not doing admin. Log each job in one row and the tracker tells you what's paid, what's still owed and what's coming up next.

Keep client details and access notes in one place, record running costs, and see your revenue, profit and hourly earnings for the year. The dashboard also breaks down revenue by service and by cleaner, so you know what's worth your time.

What it does

  • Job log with automatic Paid / Unpaid / Scheduled status
  • Client list with access notes, job count, revenue, unpaid balance and upcoming bookings
  • Extras (oven, fridge, linen) added to the job total
  • Earnings per hour for every job and for the year
  • Expense log so the dashboard shows profit, not just sales
  • Dashboard: revenue, expenses, profit, unpaid money, jobs done, hours, average per hour, jobs scheduled
  • Charts: monthly revenue vs expenses, revenue by service, hours and revenue by cleaner

What you get

  • Cleaning Business Job & Client Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
