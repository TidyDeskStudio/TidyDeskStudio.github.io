Every December it is the same: who did we gift last year, did we already send this one the hamper, and why has the gift budget gone again?

This spreadsheet gives you a gift list with a recipient dropdown, the gift, type, cost, ship-by date and status, and it shows last year's gift and cost next to this year's row with a Repeat check. A Budget tab adds up spent and still-idea gifts by group and shows On budget, Near limit or Over, a Recipients tab keeps preferences and contact details, and a Card sent and Reply received column keeps the thank-you cards in order. The dashboard adds a ship-by calendar, group spotlight, prep checklist, alerts and charts. It is a gift planning and record-keeping tool and not financial advice.

What it does

  • Gift list with recipient dropdown, gift, type, bought date, ship-by date, cost and status
  • Last year's gift and cost shown beside this year's row, with a Same as last year repeat check
  • Late, Ship soon and On track flags from the ship-by date
  • Budget tab by group with Spent, Still ideas, Last year and an On budget, Near limit or Over status
  • Recipients tab with group, contact, preferences, gifts given and total spent
  • Card sent and Reply received columns plus a Cards to send watch list
  • Dashboard with month and group dropdowns, a ship-by calendar and a tick-box prep checklist
  • Alerts plus charts for budget vs committed, this year vs last year and spending by month, with sample data and a Quick Start Guide
