Household Insurance Policy & Renewal Date Tracker

Keep every policy, premium, contact and renewal date in one place and see what is due next.

Get it on Gumroad

Home, car, life, pet, health, travel and phone cover all renew on different days, with different companies and different paperwork. Most people only find out a policy renewed when the payment leaves their account, and then cannot remember who to call or where the documents are.

This spreadsheet gives you one list for every policy. Enter the premium and how often you pay and it works out the yearly cost, counts the days to the next renewal and flags each policy as OK, Due soon or Renewal passed. Record the policy number and where the paperwork lives, keep your insurer contacts together and log claims with what was paid out. It is a record-keeping tool and does not give insurance advice.

What it does

  • Policy list with type, who or what it covers, insurer, policy number, premium and payment frequency
  • Automatic yearly cost from monthly, quarterly, six-monthly or yearly premiums
  • Days-to-renewal countdown with Due soon and Renewal passed flags
  • Document location column and a Missing paperwork alert
  • Insurers tab with phone, email, claims line, policies held and yearly cost
  • Claims log with amount claimed, status, paid out and outstanding
  • Cost Check tab to compare a renewal quote with what you pay now
  • Dashboard with biggest yearly costs, upcoming renewals, cost by type and insurer

What you get

  • Household Insurance Policy & Renewal Date Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
