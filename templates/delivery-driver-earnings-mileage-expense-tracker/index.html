You're LOSING money on every shift and can't see it.

Delivery Driver Daily Earnings, Mileage & Expense Tracker

Log each delivery shift and see your real net profit per hour, per mile and per order after fuel, wear and running costs.

Get it on Gumroad

Drivers see the money land in the app, but the app does not show what the shift cost you. Fuel, parking, tyres, servicing and your phone plan all come out of what you earned, so a busy-looking shift can leave far less than it seems.

This spreadsheet gives every shift one row. Enter hours, miles, orders, pay, tips, fuel and parking, and it works out your net profit, net per hour, per mile and per order, then flags the shift as Low earner, OK or Strong using rates you set. Overheads go on a separate tab, and the dashboard shows profit after all costs, your best platforms, days and shift types. It is a record-keeping tool and does not give tax advice.

What it does

  • Shift log with earnings, wear-and-tear cost, running cost and net profit worked out for you
  • Net per hour, per mile and per order on every shift
  • Low earner, OK and Strong flags using hourly rates you choose
  • Costs tab for phone, tyres, servicing, insurance and gear
  • Platforms tab comparing earnings, net profit, net per hour and tips share
  • Weekly Check tab that totals any week against your weekly goal
  • Dashboard with best-shift leaderboard, goal bars, monthly chart and a shift-type by month heatmap
  • Dropdowns, sample data and a Quick Start Guide included

What you get

  • Delivery Driver Daily Earnings, Mileage & Expense Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
