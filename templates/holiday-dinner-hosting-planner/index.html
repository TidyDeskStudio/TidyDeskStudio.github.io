Holiday Dinner Hosting Planner & Cooking Timeline

Pick the time you want to eat and get a cooking timeline for every dish, plus guests, shopping and budget in one place.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Big holiday dinners go wrong at the end: the turkey is resting, the stuffing still needs 35 minutes, and the oven is set to the wrong temperature for the rolls. Meanwhile you're not sure who said they'd bring dessert.

This planner works backwards from your serving time. Enter each dish's prep, cook and resting minutes and it tells you when to start prepping, when each dish goes into the oven and when it comes out, with day names for anything made ahead. If two oven dishes need different temperatures at the same time, both rows are flagged so you can adjust before the day. A Guests tab tracks RSVPs, party sizes, food preferences and who is bringing what, and the shopping list is grouped by store section with a running budget and cost per person.

What it does

  • Cooking timeline worked out backwards from your serving day and time
  • Start prep, into oven and out of oven times for every dish, including make-ahead dishes
  • Oven temperature clash alert when two dishes overlap at different temperatures
  • Guest list with RSVP, party size, food preferences and the dishes each guest is bringing
  • Shopping list by store section, linked to dishes, with Got it / To buy status
  • Budget left and cost per person from your shopping estimates
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, shopping by store section, dishes by course

What you get

  • Holiday Dinner Hosting Planner & Cooking Timeline (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
