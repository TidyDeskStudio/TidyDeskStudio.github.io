Personal Trainer Client & Session Tracker

Know exactly how many sessions each client has left - and who owes you money.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Session packages are great for cash flow but messy to track. Who has three sessions left? Did that no-show count? Has the balance been paid? This tracker answers all of it.

Log each client's package, every session and every payment. The client list shows sessions used and left, amount paid and owed, attendance rate, and a Renew soon flag when someone is close to the end of their package - the perfect moment to offer the next one. The dashboard shows revenue, sessions delivered, attendance and renewals due.

What it does

  • Client packages with session count and price
  • Session log with attendance: Attended, No-show, Late cancel, Cancelled (not charged)
  • Sessions used, sessions left, paid and owed for every client
  • Renewal alerts when a client is close to the end of their package
  • Attendance rate per client and overall
  • Dashboard: revenue, money owed, sessions delivered, attendance, active clients, hours coached, revenue per session
  • Charts: sessions by month, sessions by type, used vs left per client

What you get

  • Personal Trainer Client & Session Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
