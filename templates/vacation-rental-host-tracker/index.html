Vacation Rental Host Booking & Expense Tracker

Know what each rental really earns - after channel fees, cleaning and running costs.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

The payout figure on a booking isn't your profit. Channel fees, cleaning, linen, utilities and repairs all come out of it, and it's hard to see which property or channel is actually worth it. This tracker puts every stay and every cost in one place.

Add each booking once: dates, nightly rate, cleaning fee, channel fee and what you paid the cleaner. Nights, gross, net and status are calculated for you. Each property shows nights booked, occupancy, average nightly rate, net income, expenses and profit, and the dashboard compares months, properties and booking channels.

What it does

  • Booking log with automatic nights, gross, net payout and Upcoming / In stay / Completed status
  • Cleaning fee charged vs cleaner paid, and channel fees per booking
  • Per-property nights booked, occupancy %, average nightly rate, net income, expenses and profit
  • Expense log by property and category
  • Dashboard: gross, net, expenses, profit, nights, occupancy, average rate, upcoming stays
  • Charts: monthly net vs expenses, profit by property, results by booking channel
  • Rename channels and categories to match how you host

What you get

  • Vacation Rental Host Booking & Expense Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
