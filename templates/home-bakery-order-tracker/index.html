Home Bakery Order & Profit Tracker

Every custom order, deposit and pickup date in one place - plus what you actually earn.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Taking orders through messages and a paper diary works until two cakes are due the same morning and you can't remember who paid a deposit. This tracker keeps every order in one row and does the sums for you.

Set your deposit percentage once. Each order shows the total, the deposit due, the balance left and a clear status, and the dashboard tells you what's due for pickup this week, who still owes you, and how much profit you're making after ingredients and packaging.

What it does

  • Order log with order date, pickup date, product, details, quantity and price
  • Automatic deposit due based on your deposit %
  • Status: Awaiting deposit, Deposit paid, Paid, Complete, Balance overdue
  • Days until pickup and a count of pickups in the next 7 days
  • Expense log for ingredients, packaging and fees
  • Dashboard: sales, expenses, profit, money owed, average order, monthly chart, sales by product, where orders come from
  • Edit your own product list and order channels

What you get

  • Home Bakery Order & Profit Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
