Holiday Order Shipping Cut-Off & Parcel Tracker

See which holiday orders still need packing, which missed their shipping cut-off and which parcels are running late.

Every November the same worry starts: which orders are packed, which have shipped, and which will miss the last date to reach the customer in time? A scribbled list or an inbox search stops working once the orders pile up.

This spreadsheet keeps one row per order and works out the rest. Set the last ship date and transit days for each service you offer, and every order shows On track, Ship soon, Missed cut-off, Delayed, On the way, Late parcel or Delivered. The Cut-Offs tab shows which services are still Open or Closed, the Tasks tab tracks your holiday prep, and the dashboard adds a ship-by calendar, a service spotlight, alerts and charts. It is a record-keeping tool. Carrier dates and delivery times are your own estimates and are not guaranteed.

What it does

  • Orders tab with customer, item, region, service, value and status dropdowns
  • Cut-Offs tab with last ship date and transit days for each service you offer
  • Automatic flag on every order: On track, Ship soon, Missed cut-off, Delayed, On the way, Late parcel or Delivered
  • Expected delivery date and days left to cut-off worked out for you
  • Service windows that show Open, Closing soon or Closed
  • Holiday prep task list with owners, due dates and Overdue or Due soon flags
  • Dashboard with a ship-by calendar, service spotlight dropdown, tick-box packing steps and attention lists
  • Charts for order value by month, orders by service and orders by status, with sample data and a Quick Start Guide

What you get
