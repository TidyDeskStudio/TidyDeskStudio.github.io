Personal Chef Client, Menu & Grocery Cost Tracker

Track clients, plan weekly menus, log grocery receipts and see what every cooking day really earned.

Get it on Gumroad

Running a personal chef or meal-prep business means remembering that one client hates mushrooms, another wants lunchbox food, and that last week's shop cost more than you charged. Notes in your phone and receipts in a bag do not add up to a profit figure.

This spreadsheet keeps a profile for every client, a weekly menu planner, a grocery receipt log and a list of cooking days. Groceries are matched to each service by client and date, your own time is costed at your hourly rate, and every cooking day shows its profit, margin and cost per serving with a flag when it is thin. Dietary notes are record-keeping only, not nutrition or medical advice.

What it does

  • Client profiles with likes, dislikes, household size and notes exactly as the client told you
  • Weekly menu planner with servings, estimated cost per serving and a dislikes-checked reminder
  • Grocery receipt log by store and category, matched to each client's cooking day
  • Cooking-day log with price charged, hours, travel and supplies
  • Automatic labour cost from your hourly rate, plus cost per serving
  • Profit and margin per service with Healthy, Thin margin and Losing money flags
  • Client table showing revenue, profit and margin for each client
  • Dashboard with revenue, profit, grocery spend, payments waiting and charts by month, client and category

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
