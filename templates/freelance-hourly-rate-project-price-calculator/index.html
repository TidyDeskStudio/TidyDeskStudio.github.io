Freelance Hourly Rate & Project Price Calculator

Work out the hourly rate you actually need, then price every project from it and check what each job really paid.

Get it on Gumroad

Most freelancers set their rate by guessing, or by copying what someone else charges. Then a project runs over, the revisions pile up and the real hourly rate quietly drops below what you need to live on.

This spreadsheet starts with your own numbers: the take-home you want, tax set-aside, business costs, weeks off and billable hours. It works out a minimum and a target hourly rate, suggests a price for every project, and after the job shows the real hourly rate once actual hours and costs are in. It is a planning and record-keeping tool, not tax or financial advice.

What it does

  • Rate calculator that turns your income goal, costs, tax set-aside and time off into minimum and target hourly and day rates
  • Project quote checker: price at your target rate, lowest acceptable price and a verdict against the client's budget
  • Dashboard with a best-paying-projects leaderboard, goal progress bars, an attention list and an unpaid-work list
  • Real hourly rate for every project after direct costs, flagged On target, Under target or Below minimum
  • Hours check against your estimate, so scope creep shows up straight away
  • Fees heatmap by service and month, a rate-health ring and a pipeline from Quoted to Paid
  • Client tab with fees billed, hours and average real rate per client
  • Monthly and per-client charts, dropdowns and sample data included

  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
