Tutor Lesson & Payment Tracker

Every lesson, every rate and every unpaid fee - plus a monthly total for each family.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Tutoring admin is small but constant: who you taught, for how long, at what rate, and which families still need to pay. This tracker handles it with one row per lesson.

Set each student's hourly rate once and the fee for every lesson is calculated from its length. Charged and free cancellations are handled, unpaid lessons are totalled per student, and a statement-month total gives you each family's bill for the month in one glance. The dashboard shows earnings, hours, unpaid fees and your split by subject and student.

What it does

  • Student list with payer, contact details and individual hourly rates
  • Lesson log: date, subject, minutes, status - fee calculated automatically
  • Charged vs free cancellations
  • Paid / Unpaid status and unpaid total per family
  • Monthly statement total per student - pick the month on the Start Here tab
  • Dashboard: earnings, unpaid fees, hours and lessons taught, average hourly earnings
  • Charts: earnings and hours by month, by subject, by student

What you get

  • Tutor Lesson & Payment Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
