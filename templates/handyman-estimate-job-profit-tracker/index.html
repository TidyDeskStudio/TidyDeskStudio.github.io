Handyman Estimate & Job Profit Tracker

Price handyman jobs from labour and materials, then track every estimate until it's paid.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Working out estimates on the back of a receipt, forgetting to chase quotes and not knowing whether a job really paid once the materials came off - it all eats into your week.

This tracker builds each estimate from your hourly rate, a materials price list with your markup, and an optional trip charge. Move jobs from Estimate sent to Won, Done and Paid, and it shows what you charged, what the job cost you, profit per hour and whether you went over your estimated hours. Old estimates show Follow up and late payments show Overdue, and a Quick Estimate tab prices small jobs on the spot.

What it does

  • Estimate totals built from hours x your rate, materials with markup and a trip charge
  • Materials price list and estimate lines - update a price once and every estimate follows
  • Stages from Estimate sent to Won, Done and Lost, with Follow up, To do, Unpaid, Overdue and Paid alerts
  • Actual hours and materials per job: profit, profit per hour and hours over or under estimate
  • Optional final price to override the estimate when the scope changes
  • Quick Estimate calculator for pricing small jobs on site
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers, monthly revenue vs profit, profit by job type and estimate value by stage

What you get

  • Handyman Estimate & Job Profit Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
