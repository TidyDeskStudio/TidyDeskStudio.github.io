Subscription & Recurring Payment Tracker

Find every subscription, see what it really costs per month and per year, and get a warning before renewals and trials.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

Small monthly charges are easy to forget until they add up. A streaming service nobody watches, a free trial that quietly turned into a yearly bill, an app you opened twice. This tracker puts every subscription and repeat payment in one list so you can see the real total.

Enter the price and billing cycle and it works out the monthly and yearly cost of each one, weekly, quarterly and yearly bills included. Renewals and trials coming up in your warning window are flagged, and a Keep / Review / Cancel column helps you decide what goes. Cancelled items show what you're saving each year.

What it does

  • Monthly and yearly cost for weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly billing
  • Alerts: Renews soon, Trial ending, Cancel now, Update date, Cancelled
  • Your own warning window for renewals and free trials
  • Usage column and Keep / Review / Cancel decision for each subscription
  • Saved per year for everything you cancel
  • Dashboard: monthly and yearly spend, renewals and trials due, money on rarely used services
  • Breakdowns by category, payment method and how often each one is used

What you get

  • Subscription & Recurring Payment Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros. A record-keeping tool, not financial advice.
