Job Application Tracker

Stay on top of every application and follow-up - and learn which job sources actually reply.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

When you're applying to dozens of roles, it's easy to lose track of who you've heard from, what the next step is and which interviews went well. This tracker keeps your whole job search in one place.

Log each application in a row and update the stage as it moves. The tracker tells you when a next step is due, which applications have gone quiet and need chasing, and your response, interview and offer counts. An interview log keeps your notes and questions for the next round, and the dashboard shows which job sources are worth your time.

What it does

  • Application log with company, role, location, work type, source, salary and stage
  • Stages: Applied, Screening, Interview, Final round, Offer, Accepted, Rejected, Withdrawn
  • Follow-up alerts: Due now, Chase up, No response, Waiting (you set the days)
  • Interview log with questions asked and a 1-5 self-rating
  • Dashboard: applications, response rate, interviews, offers, active applications, next steps due
  • Charts: applications by month, where you are now, which sources get replies

What you get

  • Job Application Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
