Nonprofit Grant Application & Report Deadline Tracker

Track every grant application, award and report deadline so nothing slips through the cracks.

Get it on Payhip

When a small team applies to a dozen funders a year, deadlines live in inboxes and sticky notes. Then a final report is late, or nobody remembers what you asked a funder for last spring.

This tracker keeps funders, applications and reporting dates in one workbook. Each application shows days left and an alert (On track, Due soon, Missed, Waiting, Awarded), reports turn amber and red as their due dates near, and the dashboard shows money requested vs awarded, your award rate and what's coming up next. It is an organising tool only - it doesn't give fundraising, legal or financial advice.

What it does

  • Applications pipeline: Researching, Drafting, Submitted, Awarded, Declined, Withdrawn
  • Days-left countdown and alerts: On track, Due soon, Missed, Waiting
  • Reports tab with Upcoming, Due soon, Overdue and Submitted status
  • Funders list with applications, amount requested, amount awarded and open reports per funder
  • Dashboard: 8 headline numbers including award rate and deadlines in the next 30 days
  • Monthly requested vs awarded chart, plus breakdowns by funder type and by stage
  • Set your own warning window, currency and dashboard year

What you get

  • Nonprofit Grant Application & Report Deadline Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros.
