Business Mileage Log & Deduction Tracker

A simple mileage log that adds up your business distance and claim value for tax time.

Get it on GumroadGet it on Payhip

If you drive for work, every business trip is worth money at tax time - but only if you've written it down. This log makes it quick: date, purpose, where you went and either odometer readings or the distance.

Round trips double automatically, personal trips are kept separate, and your claim value is worked out using the rate you set (miles or km, any currency). The dashboard totals your business distance and claim for the year, by month and by purpose.

What it does

  • Trip log with odometer readings or manual distance
  • Round trip toggle doubles the distance
  • Business / personal flag - only business trips count towards your claim
  • Works in miles or km with your own claim rate and currency
  • Dashboard: business distance, claim value, trips, average trip, business share of driving, this month's claim
  • Charts by month and by purpose
  • Room for 2,000 trips

What you get

  • Business Mileage Log & Deduction Tracker (.xlsx)
  • Quick Start Guide (PDF)

Works with: Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets. No macros.
