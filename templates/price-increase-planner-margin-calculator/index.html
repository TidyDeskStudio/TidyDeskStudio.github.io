2027 Price Increase Planner & Margin Calculator

Work out the new price for every item, see the margin it gives and what a small drop in sales does to monthly profit.

Costs went up, you know prices have to follow, and you are not sure by how much. Raise too little and your margin keeps shrinking, raise too much and regulars may walk. Most owners end up guessing a flat percentage across everything.

This spreadsheet lets you list each product or service with its cost, price and monthly sales. It works out the price that reaches your target margin, or tests your own planned rise, and shows the margin, the new price rounded to your price step and the monthly profit now, if you hold prices, and after the rise, using a sales-drop guess that you set. It is a record-keeping and planning tool, not financial or pricing advice.

What it does

  • Item list with automatic cost for next year, target price and new price rounded to a step you choose
  • Enter your own planned rise per item or let the sheet suggest the price that hits your target margin
  • Margin now and margin after, plus a Below floor, Big rise, Ready or Hold check on every item
  • Monthly profit now, if you hold prices and after the rise, using your own sales-drop assumption
  • Cost Changes log with percentage change and Large, Moderate or None size flags
  • Rise Test tab with the break-even sales loss for a single price
  • Dashboard with biggest-gain leaderboard, category chart, supplier heatmap and goal bars
  • Editable categories and suppliers lists
