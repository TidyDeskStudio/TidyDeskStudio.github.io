Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

TidyDesk Studio sells downloadable spreadsheet templates and publishes free guides. This page explains what information we handle.

This website

This site has no accounts, forms, comments or advertising trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security. We do not sell or share personal information.

Purchases

Purchases happen on Gumroad or Payhip. They process your name, email and payment details under their own privacy policies; we receive only what is needed to deliver your download and answer support questions.

Social media and Pinterest

We use the official Pinterest, Instagram and Facebook APIs only to publish and manage posts on our own accounts and to read basic statistics about those posts. We do not collect data about other users.

Contact

Questions about your data? Message us through the store where you bought, or on our social accounts, and we will help.
